COMMON CONCERNS

We hear you.
Here are the answers.

"I've used the same system for 10 years. Is switching really worth it?" — The most common question we get
I've used Word and Excel for years. Why change now?

Because your practice has likely grown to the point where those tools are costing you time you're not billing. Lineal imports your existing Word notes in the onboarding wizard. Most members are up and running in under an hour. And the first time a client requests an amendment and you pull up the full research trail in 3 seconds — you'll wonder how you managed without it.

Does Lineal support Evidence Explained citation formats?

Yes. EE citations are built into the research log entry form. Select a source category (census, vital record, church record, land record, newspaper, etc.), fill in the fields, and the citation is formatted automatically. We support all major EE source categories from the 3rd edition.

I work with clients who aren't tech-savvy. Can they use the portal?

Absolutely. The client portal is just a link you send. They click it, see their project status, and drag-and-drop their documents. No account creation, no passwords. It looks professional and takes your client less than 2 minutes to figure out. We've tested it with clients in their 70s and 80s.

What about client confidentiality?

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Client files are stored in a private bucket accessible only to you and your clients. We don't share, sell, or analyze your client data. Your research is yours. We also support data export at any time — your records are never held hostage.

Is this for APG members only?

Not at all. Lineal works for any professional genealogist — whether you hold an AG, CG, or no formal credential. If you're billing clients for genealogy research, Lineal is for you. That said, APG members get a 20% lifetime discount (use code APG at checkout).
